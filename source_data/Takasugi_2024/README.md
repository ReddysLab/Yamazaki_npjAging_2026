Public source-data reference for Takasugi et al. 2024 aging multi-omics analyses.

Final manuscript analyses use public bulk RNA-seq and proteomics measurements across eight mouse tissues at 6, 15, 24, and 30 months. `Figure_4_multiomics_long.csv.gz` is the gzip-compressed replicate-level table consumed by the R workflow; `Figure_4_trajectory_features.csv` contains the corresponding feature annotations used for panel construction.
